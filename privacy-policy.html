Privacy Policy — LabPy IDE

Developer: Shehzad Ali
Contact: starlancer789@gmail.com

Data collection

LabPy IDE does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app works fully offline: all code you write, run, and save stays on your device in the app's private storage. No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no accounts, and no data is sent to any server.

Local storage

Scripts you save ("My Scripts") and your settings (theme, font size, language) are stored only on your device. You can delete saved scripts at any time from within the app, and clearing the app's data removes everything.

Permissions

LabPy IDE requests no sensitive permissions and does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files outside its own storage (except when you explicitly choose to open or save a .py file).

Children

The app is an educational tool and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Contact

If you email the developer, your email address is used only to reply to you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address and inside the app (About & Privacy).
